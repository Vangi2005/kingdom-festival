📜 Kingdom Codex: Aldermere

Realm: the Kingdom of Aldermere, a green valley of orchards, rivers and old magic.

Ruler: Queen Isolde IV, the Lantern Queen. Seat: Highspire Keep, the castle on the horizon.

Folk: humans, halflings, dwarves, wandering bards and one very polite dragon.

Festival: the Harvestmoon Festival, held on the 21st of Emberfall, Year 812 of the Third Age.

Why we celebrate: ten years ago the Long Winter gripped the land until Queen Isolde returned the Sunstone to the fountain. Each year the realm thanks the sun, trades its harvest and keeps torches burning all night so the cold never returns.

Your quest: find all 9 relics: 8 on the merchant stalls plus the Sunstone Shard above the fountain. Walk up to a relic to inspect it, then press E (or click) to collect it. In VR, grab it with the trigger and hold it at your face for a second. Collect all nine and the Queen will present you with the Harvestmoon Trophy.

Tip: drag the time slider to dusk; the torches and castle windows glow. Watch the jugglers.
